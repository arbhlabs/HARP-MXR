MixDock for Mac - first open

MixDock is free and open source but not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first open.
You only have to allow it once.

1. Drag MixDock into Applications.
2. Double-click MixDock in Applications. When macOS says it cannot verify the app, click Done.
3. Allow it:
   - macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
     Security and click "Open Anyway" next to MixDock, then confirm.
   - macOS 14 (Sonoma): right-click (Control-click) MixDock in Applications, choose Open, then Open.
   - Or, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MixDock.app
4. Xbox controller: hold the pairing button on top of the controller until the Xbox logo flashes fast,
   then pick "Xbox Wireless Controller" in System Settings > Bluetooth.
5. USB DJ controller: plug it in, press MIDI in MixDock, then LEARN next to a function and move the control.
6. To mix Spotify or a browser (macOS 14.4+): start the music, then pick it under SOURCE on a deck.
   macOS asks to allow audio recording the first time: click Allow. Nothing is recorded or saved.
